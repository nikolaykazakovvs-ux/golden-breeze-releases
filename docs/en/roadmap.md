# Golden Breeze: Capabilities & Roadmap

**Language / Мова / Язык:** **English** · [Українська (docs/uk/roadmap.md)](../uk/roadmap.md) · [Русский (docs/roadmap.md)](../roadmap.md)

**Showcase snapshot as of November 21, 2026** (available `v0.17.2-alpha` release in the `stable` channel published on 20.11.2026; no `v0.17` live acceptance report is published on the showcase; historical `v0.14.0-alpha` acceptance snapshot as of 12.11.2026). Golden Breeze is built as a multi-agent trading platform: collaborative work of specialized AI agents across the trading workflow — from market analysis and trading decisions to execution, position management, and risk control under user governance. Research, strategy development, versioning, and isolated strategy verification serve as a built-in engineering workflow of the platform, providing a transparent path from hypothesis to controlled execution.

## How to Read Statuses

- **In Release (`v0.17.2-alpha`, `stable`)** — capability is included in the published `FirstInstall` installer (`v0.17.2-alpha`) according to the release notes and its source revision; end-to-end live acceptance is recorded separately by specific scenarios and account boundaries.
- **Verified Scope & Acceptance Boundaries** — specifies in which environment a scenario was verified: the cloud Windows environment for the `stable` product installer, the local Windows x64 rig on the dedicated acceptance build with an `FTMO-Demo` account, calculation/dry-run evaluation of an ordinary strategy, or acceptance/diagnostic demo trades, as well as which follow-up checks are scheduled separately.
- **Historical Acceptance Snapshot (`v0.14.0-alpha`, 12.11.2026)** — checks performed for the previous `v0.14.0-alpha` installer; they are not carried over to the `v0.17.2-alpha` installer.
- **Planned / Design Stage** — target roadmap tracks and architectural modules currently in the design phase.
- **Deferred** — standalone future capability or expense intentionally set aside so it does not block the current path.

> **Important Boundary:** publishing the `FirstInstall` installer, passing automated CI, and verifying auto-update confirm build delivery, while live functional acceptance is tracked strictly by verified scenarios and environments. The platform supports connecting both `Demo` and `Real` accounts in MT5 (the mandatory "demo-only" guard applies exclusively to the dedicated diagnostic test route), while live trading acceptance of `v0.14.0-alpha` was historically completed on `FTMO-Demo`, no `v0.17.2-alpha` live acceptance report is published on the showcase, and completed live trading acceptance on a `Real` account is not claimed.

## Planned Tracks & Design Stage

- **Multiple Trading Executors & Portfolio Management (Planned).** Scaling multi-agent trading: parallel coordination of specialized trading agents across multiple instruments and sessions, capital allocation, enforcement of verified prop-firm risk rules, end-to-end portfolio drawdown control, and market regime analytics.
- **Platform Catalog Expansion & Dedicated TradeLocker Integration (Planned).** Expanding the unified registry of connected trading platforms and prop firms with transparent capability cards for each adapter. **TradeLocker** connector integration is scheduled as a standalone planned task and does not block current MT5 acceptance.
- **Saved Trading Agent Workflows & Deep Validation (Planned).** Repeatable multi-step routines for readiness checks, market study, enforcement of verified prop-firm rules, task delegation, and risk governance.
- **Licensing & Access Tiers (Design Stage).** Architectural design of access keys and closed beta testing modes (`Free` / `Pro` / `Tester`), device binding, and capability tier separation without mixing licensing with trading permissions (plans, pricing, and sales terms are not yet approved).

## Deferred Decisions

- **Expanded Studio & Deep Historical Backtesting (Deferred).** A standalone built-in code editor for manual programming in Studio and a custom historical backtesting engine with dataset management are deferred so as not to delay core trading and multi-agent workflows (baseline strategy code generation in chat and isolated synthetic-bar verification were implemented in `0.13–0.14-alpha`, and the signal check on history (Replay) in `v0.15`; Replay does not replace a backtest).
- **Authenticode Certificate & Custom Domain (Deferred by Project Decision).** Purchasing a paid Windows Authenticode publisher certificate for `.exe` binaries and a dedicated custom domain is deferred until the product reaches profitability. Meanwhile, update delivery is protected over HTTPS (TLS) and by cryptographic manifest signatures (Ed25519), and downloaded installer integrity is verified via `SHA-256`.
